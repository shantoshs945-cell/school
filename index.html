A little about us

Good at learning.
Even better at being you.

VEDA School is for students in Class 1 through Class 10. Across these school years, learning grows with every child: building confidence in the early classes, exploring new interests in the middle years, and strengthening subject knowledge and study habits in Classes 9 and 10.

Talk to our admissions team →
Can we visit before applying?

Absolutely. Book a personal visit for a relaxed tour of the campus, a chance to meet a teacher, and time to ask all your questions.

How do I ask about admissions and age groups?

Send us a WhatsApp message or call the admissions team. We’ll talk through the current classes, availability, and next steps for your child.

What should we bring to our first visit?

Just bring your questions and your child if you’d like. We’ll show you around and help you understand whether the school feels right for your family.

Can I arrange an appointment on WhatsApp?

Yes—use the WhatsApp button and tell us your preferred day, time, and your child’s age or grade. Our team can help find a suitable slot.
